Dickinson College: $93,706 a Year. And This Is What’s on the Shelves in Carlisle.

THE PRICE OF ADMISSION

The tuition bill is impressive. So are the kratom displays.

Dickinson College lists $71,100 in tuition and an estimated $93,706 total annual cost of attendance for 2026–27. That is its published cost before financial aid. See Dickinson’s published figures.

Families examine classrooms, residence halls and dining plans before choosing a college. They should also take a hard look at what is being sold in the town where their children will spend four years.

WHAT PARENTS SHOULD KNOW

Plant-derived does not mean harmless.

Kratom contains mitragynine, an alkaloid that acts at opioid receptors. Kratom use has been associated with dependence, withdrawal, seizures, liver injury and other serious adverse events. Its botanical origin is not a guarantee of safety.

The question is not whether kratom comes from a plant. The question is why its risks are so easy to overlook while its packages are so easy to find.

Does the college address kratom by name in its substance-use education? Does it warn students about dependence and withdrawal? If students experience problems involving kratom, do they know where to turn?

THE BOTTOM LINE

Nearly $94,000 a year. Have you looked beyond the campus tour?

A college can show parents its best classrooms, prettiest buildings and proudest traditions. The kratom displays in Carlisle deserve a look, too.

Before choosing a college, ask what is sold around it. Then ask what the college is doing to make sure students understand the risks.
