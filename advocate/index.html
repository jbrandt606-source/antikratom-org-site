Open Truth: Advocates Edition

Kratom's defenders have spent years walking into statehouses armed with testimonials, miracle recoveries, borrowed scientific authority, attacks on medical examiners, and an apparently bottomless supply of reasons why kratom deserves the credit when something goes right and none of the blame when something goes wrong. Open Truth simply applies their favorite concept—scrutiny—to the people doing the selling. No rumors required. Their testimony, publications, advocacy materials, organizational ties and own words provide more than enough material. We didn't write the record. We just stopped politely looking away.
