What is being funded?

WSU's announcement describes research into botanical kratom in relation to chronic pain and sleep, including preclinical work and a planned human sleep study involving chronic low back pain. The researchers plan to use an authenticated, purified preparation. This is research into unanswered questions—not evidence that kratom is an approved or proven treatment.

Primary source: Washington State University, October 26, 2026 ↗

A grant announcement is not a scientific result. The approved plan, safeguards, budget and deliverables deserve public examination.

What the federal abstract actually proposes

1

Chronic pain mouse model

Test effects of kratom on chronic pain-related adverse outcomes.

2

Healthy adult pharmacokinetics

Dose-escalation study of two commonly used kratom formulations.

3

Patients with chronic low back pain

Controlled laboratory study assessing quality of life and objective sleep measures.

The federal abstract also describes pharmacokinetic/pharmacodynamic modeling. It does not establish kratom's clinical efficacy. Read the primary-source abstract ↗

Sample taxpayer inquiry

Copy and personalize this inquiry. Send it through the appropriate NIH public inquiry or FOIA channel; a FOIA request should clearly identify the records sought.

Subject: Request for Information — WSU $7.5 Million Kratom Research Award

To the National Institutes of Health:

I am requesting information concerning the reported $7.5 million federal research award to Washington State University to study botanical kratom, chronic pain and sleep, announced October 26, 2026.

Please identify the award number, administering institute, approved project aims, publicly releasable budget details, planned safety monitoring, conflict-of-interest safeguards and expected milestones or deliverables.

I also request clarification of how findings involving an authenticated, purified kratom preparation are expected to inform understanding of commercially available kratom products.

I recognize that research can be valuable even when a substance has significant risks. My concern is that public expenditures should have a documented rationale, transparent oversight and meaningful outcomes.

Please provide relevant public records or direct me to the appropriate office for obtaining them.

Thank you for your consideration.

Sincerely,
[Your Name]
[City, State]
