Circle K customers have a simple request: please stop selling kratom and kratom-derived products in Circle K stores. A national convenience-store brand can choose what belongs on its shelves. We are asking Circle K to choose public health.

Take two minutes. Send one message.

Use Circle K's official customer contact form and tell corporate leadership that you do not want kratom sold at Circle K. Be respectful, be specific, and ask for a company-wide policy.

WRITE TO CIRCLE K USE THE SAMPLE LETTER

What We Are Asking Circle K to Do

We are asking Circle K Stores Inc. to adopt a clear national retail policy ending the sale of kratom, kratom extracts, mitragynine products, 7-hydroxymitragynine products, and other kratom-derived products at company-operated stores, and to apply equivalent brand standards to franchised locations where its agreements permit.

This is not complicated: Circle K does not have to wait for Congress to decide what belongs on Circle K shelves.

Why Write?

Retailers pay attention to repeated, specific customer concerns. Circle K's own contact page invites comments and suggestions about products. A short message from a real customer is more useful than an angry social-media post because it reaches the company through the channel it created for product feedback.

1

Open Circle K's official contact page

Use the product/customer feedback form so your request goes directly into the company's customer-care system.

2

Tell them exactly what you want

Ask Circle K to stop retail sales of kratom and kratom-derived products nationwide. If you have seen kratom at a particular location, include the store address or city.

3

Keep it professional

A calm customer request is harder to dismiss. Explain why the issue matters to you and ask for a written response describing Circle K's policy.

Sample Letter

Copy this, personalize one or two sentences, and send it through Circle K's official contact form.

Subject: Please End Retail Sales of Kratom at Circle K

To Circle K Corporate Leadership,

I am writing as a customer to ask Circle K to stop selling kratom and kratom-derived products in its U.S. stores.

Kratom products contain opioid-active compounds and have become increasingly available in convenience-store settings. Whatever one's position on broader drug policy, Circle K is not required to sell this product category. A national retailer can set a higher standard for what it chooses to place on its shelves.

I am asking Circle K Stores Inc. to adopt a company-wide policy ending retail sales of kratom, kratom extracts, mitragynine products, 7-hydroxymitragynine products, and other kratom-derived products at company-operated locations, and to extend that standard to franchised stores where possible.

Please let me know whether Circle K will review this product category and consider removing it from retail sale.

Thank you for your attention to this request.

Sincerely,
[Your Name]
[City, State]
